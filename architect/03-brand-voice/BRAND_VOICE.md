# Venturo brand voice

Extracted from Brand Guide v1.2 (March 2025). This folder had no separate voice file in the drop; the guide is the source.

## How we sound

Bold and daring, kind and excited. We take risks, find wonder in ordinary towns, and treat humour as part of adventure. We share useful tips without sounding like a brochure. We welcome people in. We are here to have fun.

Voice words: **Bold · Magical · Humorous · Insightful · Daring**

## Brand statement

We’re Venturo. We provide a way for curious adventurers to easily find things to do and places to go in their local areas. We’re bold, brave and beyond excited to explore our towns and make memories with like-minded thrill-seekers & travellers.

## Approved taglines

Do not rewrite these.

- Quality time is our love language
- Your next adventure awaits
- Explore, connect, thrive
- Find your next Thrill
- Expand your comfort zone
- life’s an adventure

Do not mix a brand tagline with a campaign-specific line on the same lockup.

## Writing style

- Headlines: short, clear, hook the reader
- Use `&` instead of `and`
- Headlines: Title Case, not sentence case
- Sentences: period at the end, capitalise the first word
- No spaces around em-dashes
- Do not end a bullet in a period unless the bullet has more than one sentence
- Do not hyphenate paragraphs
- Phone: `123-456-7890` (hyphens, no parentheses)
- Time: 24-hour with a colon (`13:30`). No AM/PM. `00:00` = midnight, `12:00` = noon
- Time ranges: en-dash, not “to” / “through”
- Write `24-hour`, not `24 hour`
- Days: full word (`Monday – Thursday`)
- Addresses: only the province is abbreviated
- Spelling: South African English (`colour`, `grey`)

## Do / don’t

| Do | Don’t |
| --- | --- |
| Sound like a curious local guide | Sound corporate, salesy, or sarcastic at someone’s expense |
| Promise easy discovery of real things to do | Overclaim (“the only app you’ll ever need”) |
| Talk about people, memories, and the twist that makes it fun | Talk only about features and discounts |
| Keep energy high and specific | Use empty hype with no place, activity, or feeling |
